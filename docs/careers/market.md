Checked 20 November 2026. These are dated observations from different datasets. Use them to choose roles to investigate, then compare actual responsibilities, location and entry requirements.

## Open positions at tech companies

The readable portion of [Lenny Rachitsky’s report, 10 November 2026](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-tech-job-market-late) uses TrueUp’s global coverage of over 9,000 tech companies.

| Category | Reported observation |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Openings up 2.5% over the past year |
| FDE | About 1,500 openings, 4.6 times the year-earlier level |
| Product design | Openings down 28% over the past year |
| Product management | Openings 75% above the March 2023 low, a different comparison period |

Lenny notes that some FDE growth may reflect renamed roles. These figures measure openings, not completed hires, salaries or competition per vacancy. They do not establish a growth rate for Russia or for Product Builder.

### Specialties in Lenny’s public follow-up

Lenny’s [11 November comparison](https://x.com/lennysan/status/2120640209779884381) was checked through its [public mirrored transcript](https://nitter.cf/lennysan/status/2120640209779884381) and the matching dated entry in [Xholic’s public cache](https://xholic.ai/tools/x-profile-analytics/lennysan/). | Job title | Openings compared with a year earlier |
|---|---:|
| Forward deployed engineer | 4.6× |
| Hardware engineer | 2× |
| AI engineer | +46% |
| Security engineer | +34% |
| Front-end engineer | −51% |
| iOS/Android engineer | −46% |
| UX researcher | −45% |

Multipliers express the resulting level. A 4.6× level corresponds to a 360% increase, not 460%. Fast percentage growth does not establish a large market or an easy transition.

## A separate US postings series

[Indeed Hiring Lab, 12 November 2026](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/11/12/2026-q3-us-tech-labor-market-update/) reports these changes through **23 October 2026**.

| Indeed US category | Year on year | Since 1 February 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|
| All postings | +3.1% | +3.7% |
| Software development | +23.8% | −19.2% |

The Indeed occupation series and TrueUp’s global tech-company sample have different coverage, categories and dates. Their percentages should remain separate. Recovery from a recent low can coexist with postings below an older baseline.

## Put the evidence to work

Choose two roles and inspect their requirements before choosing a course. For FDE, check implementation ownership and customer responsibilities. For Product Builder, inspect what the team expects you to build and how it checks usefulness. **Product Builder is not a separately measured category in these sources.** PM statistics cannot establish its market size.

Compare your own evidence against the [FDE requirements](fde.md#role-requirements) or the [Product Builder route](product-builder.md). The practical question is which responsibility you can demonstrate and which gap needs a project or further experience.

Which roles have become harder to fill where you work, and which responsibilities are involved? Which skills helped people move into them? Share a dated public source through [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), with location and role scope. Personal observations remain observations rather than market totals.
